# BGV Market in India — Competitor Analysis
**Prepared by:** Nallapati Hima Tejaswini  
**Domain:** GTM Research | Background Verification Industry  
**Purpose:** Market research project analyzing India's BGV industry 
and identifying positioning opportunities for modern verification platforms.

## What's Inside
- Industry overview and market size
- Competitor profiles — AuthBridge, IDfy, SpringVerify
- Comparison table
- Where a modern BGV platform can win
- ICP recommendation and GTM positioning suggestion

## Key Finding
RecriAuth should position as *"The fastest and most startup-friendly 
BGV platform for modern hiring"* — targeting AI startups, SaaS 
companies, and GCC hiring teams where competitors are weakest.
